> A curated list of resources for medical AI, clinical deep learning, medical image analysis, biomedical natural language processing, and diagnostic algorithm frameworks.

Medical artificial intelligence applies machine learning, computer vision, and foundation models to clinical imaging, genomic sequencing, pathology, and decision support systems to enhance diagnostic accuracy and care delivery. This list is intended for medical AI researchers, clinical data scientists, biomedical engineers, and healthcare software developers.

## Medical Imaging & Computer Vision

Deep learning frameworks, image segmentation toolkits, and computer vision pipelines optimized for DICOM, NIfTI, and histopathology data.

- [MONAI (Medical Open Network for AI)](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) — PyTorch-based, open-source framework for deep learning in healthcare imaging and spatial medical data analysis.
- [nnU-Net](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet) — Self-configuring framework for medical image segmentation that automatically adapts preprocessing, network architecture, and training pipelines.
- [TorchXRayVision](https://github.com/mlmed/torchxrayvision) — Open-source library containing pre-trained chest X-ray models and datasets for diagnostic task evaluation.
- [DeepHiPS (Deep Histopathology Segmentation)](https://github.com/pathology-ai) — Deep learning pipelines for digital pathology slide analysis, cell segmentation, and tissue classification.

## Biomedical NLP & Medical LLMs

Language models, entity recognition engines, and domain-adapted LLMs fine-tuned on medical literature, EHR notes, and clinical knowledge graphs.

- [BioBERT](https://github.com/dmis-lab/biobert) — Pre-trained biomedical language representation model for biomedical text mining and named entity recognition.
- [Med-PaLM & Medical LLM Benchmarks](https://github.com/google-research/medical-llm) — Frameworks and prompts for evaluating foundation models on medical licensing examinations and clinical QA.
- [Clinical-BERT](https://github.com/EmilyAlsentzer/clinicalBERT) — Domain-specific transformer architecture fine-tuned on clinical notes from intensive care records.
- [BioNeMo Framework](https://github.com/NVIDIA/BioNeMo) — Generative AI framework for biomolecular discovery, protein structure prediction, and small molecule design.

## Clinical Decision Support Systems

Architectures, rule engines, and predictive analytics tools for integrating AI inferences into clinical workflows and EHR interfaces.

- [Fast-Path-CDS](https://github.com/cds-hooks) — CDS Hooks implementation guide for triggering AI-driven decision support cards within electronic health record systems.
- [OpenCDS Engine](https://github.com/opencds) — Multi-platform clinical decision support engine providing standards-based inference capabilities over patient data.

## Open Datasets & Benchmarks

Curated, open-access medical image repositories and clinical benchmark datasets for training diagnostic algorithms.

### Radiology & Imaging Datasets

- [CheXpert Dataset](https://github.com/stanfordmlgroup/chexpert) — Large dataset of chest radiographs with automated chest radiologist report labels for diagnostic model evaluation.
- [TCIA (The Cancer Imaging Archive)](https://github.com/microservices-tcia) — Public archive of medical images of cancer accessible for clinical AI research and image analysis.

### Clinical Text & Multimodal Benchmarks

- [MIMIC-IV Data Ecosystem](https://github.com/MIT-LCP/mimic-code) — De-identified clinical dataset encompassing emergency department and intensive care unit patient stays.
- [MedQA Benchmark](https://github.com/jair-orlando/MedQA) — Free-form multiple-choice question dataset collected from professional medical board examinations across languages.
